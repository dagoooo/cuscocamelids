Authentic Hand-Woven Textile Heritage

A great benefit of visiting the sanctuary is the opportunity to see traditional weaving and support families who preserve this delicate heritage.

Genuine Organic Process

At the weaving center, local artisans demonstrate how native plants, roots, and minerals are utilized to dye high-quality alpaca and sheep fibers naturally, entirely avoiding synthetic chemicals.

Artisan Market

The shop on site features 100% authentic backstrap-loom pieces. Purchasing these high-quality rugs, table runners, and clothing directly benefits the artisan weavers and keeps their heritage sustainable.
